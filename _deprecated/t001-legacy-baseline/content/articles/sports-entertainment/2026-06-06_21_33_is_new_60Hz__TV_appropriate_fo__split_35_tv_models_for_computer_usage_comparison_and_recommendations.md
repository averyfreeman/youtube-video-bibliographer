### Smart Bypass and Quick Startup Options

All of these models allow you to set a "Power On Behavior" to boot directly to a specific HDMI input, effectively bypassing the smart interface for your PC usage. Here's how to configure this feature for each model:

| Model | Bypass Method | Steps to Configure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pioneer (Roku) | Default Input | Settings > Antenna & Inputs > Default Input > Select HDMI 1 |
| TCL (Google TV) | Power On Behavior | Settings > System > Power & Energy > Power on behavior > Last used input |
| Hisense (Google TV) | Power On Behavior | Settings > System > Power & Energy > Power on behavior > Last used input |

### HDMI 2.1 and Refresh Rate Comparison

None of these models support a native refresh rate higher than 60Hz in the 43-inch size. While larger versions of the TCL Q6 feature 120Hz/144Hz panels, the 43-inch variant is limited to 60Hz. Here's a summary of the HDMI 2.1 support and refresh rates for each model:

| TV Model | Native Refresh Rate | HDMI 2.1 Port Count | Notable Gaming Specs |

| Pioneer PN43-R851-26U | 60Hz | 3 Ports | Includes 1 eARC port; high contrast for the price |

### Mouse Responsiveness and Motion Trails

The concerns about mouse trails and responsiveness are valid, especially since budget TVs historically struggled with high input latency for desktop use. Here's a summary of the mouse responsiveness and motion trails for each model:

* **Hisense A6 Series (A6N):** Very low input lag (~9.3ms) in Game Mode, but users report that "mousing was painful" until "Instant Game Response" was enabled.
* **TCL S4 Series (S450G):** Smooth cursor movement at 60Hz, with measured input lag around 11ms in Game Mode.
* **TCL Q6 Series (Q651G):** Low video lag, but some users report a significant audio delay (~150ms) when using Game or PC modes.
* **Pioneer PN43-R851-26U:** Generally well-regarded for its price, but uses slower internal processing (SOC), which can make menu navigation feel sluggish.

### Summary Recommendation

Based on the analysis, here's a summary recommendation for each model:

* **For pure responsiveness:** The Hisense A6N is the best choice if you're most concerned about mouse feel, as long as you enable "Instant Game Response".
* **For reliable desktop work:** The TCL S4 Series is a solid balanced option with low lag and decent motion handling.
* **A Note on 60Hz:** Dropping from a high-refresh monitor to 60Hz can feel less fluid, but sitting further back (like your 4ft desk setup) and animations will appear smoother.

Ultimately, the choice of TV model depends on your specific needs and priorities. Be sure to consider the key factors that affect performance and usability, and don't hesitate to explore additional options to find the best fit for your use case.
