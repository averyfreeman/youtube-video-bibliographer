Tiebreaker Rules for the 2026 FIFA World Cup
=============================================

In the event of teams finishing level on points in the group stage of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, a specific sequence of criteria is applied to break the tie. The regulations for the 2026 tournament have been updated, with a shift in priority from overall goal difference to head-to-head results as the primary decider.

### Tiebreaker Sequence

The tiebreaker rules are applied in the following strict order:

1. **Head-to-Head Criteria**: These rules focus strictly on the matches played between the specific teams that are tied in points. The head-to-head criteria include:
	* Head-to-head points: Most points obtained in the group matches between the tied teams.
	* Head-to-head goal difference: Best goal difference resulting from matches between the tied teams.
	* Head-to-head goals scored: Most goals scored in the matches between the tied teams.
	* Note: If more than two teams are tied, and applying the above steps successfully separates one team but leaves others still tied, the head-to-head steps are completely restarted from the beginning exclusively for the remaining tied teams.

2. **Overall Group Criteria**: If teams remain completely deadlocked after looking at their head-to-head records, the focus shifts to their performances across the entire group stage:
	* Overall goal difference: Highest total goals scored minus total goals conceded across all three group matches.
	* Overall goals scored: Total number of goals scored across all three group matches.

3. **Disciplinary Record (Fair Play)**: If teams are still identical, the team with the highest team conduct score wins the tiebreaker. Points are deducted for cards accumulated across all group matches as follows:
	* Yellow card: -1 point
	* Indirect red card (two yellow cards): -3 points
	* Direct red card: -4 points
	* Yellow card followed by a direct red card: -5 points

4. **Final Decider**: If teams cannot be separated by any of the athletic or disciplinary benchmarks above, the tie is broken by the team's standing in the official FIFA World Ranking. This objective metric replaces the random "drawing of lots" utilized in older tournaments.
