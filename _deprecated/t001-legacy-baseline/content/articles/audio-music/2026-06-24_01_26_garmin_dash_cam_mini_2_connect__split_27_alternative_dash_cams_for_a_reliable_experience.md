### 4. Format Your MicroSD Card

Corrupted video files often cause the camera’s operating system to freeze or crash when it tries to connect to the app. To fix this, format the memory card directly on the camera. Press and hold the save/action button (on the side or front) for 8 seconds until the light changes color, then release it to format the card.

Alternative Dash Cams for a Hassle-Free Experience
------------------------------------------------

If you're looking for a more straightforward and reliable dash cam experience, consider the following alternatives:

### Screen-Based Options

* **VIOFO A119 V3**: A reliable and inexpensive dash cam with a built-in 2-inch screen, allowing you to change settings and replay video directly on the device. It records in 2K resolution (1440p) and uses a supercapacitor for heat resistance.
* **70mai Smart Dash Cam 1S or M300**: An ultra-budget option with a simple design and decent app connectivity. However, it lacks a screen and may require more frequent app checks.
* **Rove R2-4K**: A feature-packed alternative with a screen, 4K recording, and built-in GPS and Wi-Fi.

### Screenless Options

* **VIOFO WM1**: A reliable and inexpensive screenless dash cam with a physical Wi-Fi button, simple app connectivity, and 2K resolution (1440p) recording.
* **Thinkware F70 Pro**: A reliable alternative with Bluetooth pairing, audio feedback, and a wide 140° field of view.
* **70mai Smart Dash Cam 1S or M300**: A budget-friendly option with simplicity and decent app connectivity, but may lack some features and video quality.

Field of View Comparison
-------------------------

All three screenless alternatives have a wide field of view, capturing the entire hood of your car and adjacent lanes:

* **Thinkware F70 Pro**: 140° field of view, matching the Garmin Dash Cam Mini 2's industry-standard wide-angle lens.
* **70mai M300**: 140° field of view, identical to the Garmin and Thinkware.
* **VIOFO WM1**: 135° field of view, slightly narrower than the Garmin but with a higher-resolution 2K sensor for sharper details.

Conclusion
----------

If you're experiencing connectivity issues with your Garmin Dash Cam Mini 2, try the troubleshooting steps outlined above. Whether you prioritize a screen-based or screenless design, there's a reliable and inexpensive option available to suit your requirements.
