### Popular Large-Format Controllers for Mixxx

When building a separate-platter setup for Mixxx, I consider various large-format media players, CD players, or modular platter controllers that support HID and MIDI mappings. Here are some popular options, categorized by their connection type and design:

#### 1. Pioneer CDJ & XDJ Series (HID / MIDI Control)

Pioneer's standard club layout is the benchmark for large-format separate players. Mixxx includes robust community mappings for several generations of these decks, including:

* Pioneer CDJ-2000 / CDJ-2000 Nexus: Widely supported via advanced HID mappings, allowing high-resolution platter tracking, tactile transport buttons, and loop controls.
* Pioneer CDJ-900 / CDJ-850: Excellent choice for a budget-conscious, standard-size platter layout, outputting stable MIDI clocks over USB.
* Pioneer XDJ-1000 (MK1 & MK2): Popular choice for those who prefer pure digital setups without CD drives, offering precise scratching and pitch-bending responses over standard USB MIDI.

#### 2. Denon DJ Prime Series (Advanced MIDI / Hybrid)

Denon players are highly regarded for their feature density and performance value, including:

* Denon DJ SC5000 / SC6000 Prime: Premium, large-format media players offering robust physical controllers with dual-layer functionality, allowing me to run two independent Mixxx virtual decks from a single physical player over USB MIDI.
* Denon DN-SC2000: A legacy compact modular deck that remains highly popular in the Mixxx community, providing dual-layer control over two decks simultaneously with high-resolution platter feedback.

#### 3. Native Instruments Traktor Kontrol S4 / S8 Modded & Denon SC3900 (Motorized Platters)

For a real-vinyl feel via heavy spinning platters, I consider:

* Denon DJ SC3900: A legendary hybrid media player featuring a 9-inch real vinyl motorized platter, with an integrated MIDI mode that maps directly to Mixxx.
* Traktor Kontrol D2 / S8 (Modular Layouts): Popular large-format modular controllers for Mixxx, using touch-strips and large screens to handle track manipulation and align with advanced multi-deck configurations.

#### 4. Any High-End Media Player via DVS (Timecode Mode)

If a player doesn't have a pre-built MIDI map in the Mixxx Controller Forum, I can use it through DVS (Digital Vinyl System) mode. This involves:

* Using club-standard monsters like the Pioneer CDJ-3000 or Denon SC6000.
* Loading a Serato or Traktor timecode audio file onto a USB drive or CD inside the unit.
* Routing the player's RCA audio outputs into a Mixxx-compatible sound card.
* Mixxx will track the platter movements with sub-millisecond accuracy, bypassing the need for a complex USB MIDI script altogether.

By exploring these options and configurations, I can find the perfect large-format controller to enhance my Mixxx performance and take my digital DJing to the next level.
