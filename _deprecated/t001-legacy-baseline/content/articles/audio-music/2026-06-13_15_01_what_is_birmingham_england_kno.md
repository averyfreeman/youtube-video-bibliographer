Birmingham, England, is most famous as the powerhouse of the Industrial Revolution, earning its nickname as "the workshop of the world." Today, the UK's "Second City" is renowned for its rich cultural diversity, musical legends, vibrant arts scene, and extensive canal network.
Birmingham, and the wider West Midlands, have a fierce reputation in the drum and bass (D&B) scene. The city is especially legendary for its massive contribution to the raw, high-energy Jump-Up subgenre. This reputation is built on the foundations laid by talented producers and DJs from the region.

Notable artists from Birmingham include K Jah, a highly respected producer with a career spanning over a decade, known for fusing a heavy jungle-reggae influence with rolling, intricate basslines on labels like Serial Killaz and Liondub International. Total Recall, real name Darren Marsh, is a Birmingham native who has released heavy tracks on legendary labels like Playaz and Full Cycle, and is widely known as the genre's premier professional one-armed artist. Jaybee (UK) is a rising stalwart in the West Midlands rave circuit, known for his heavyweight synth work, jump-up basslines, and international touring. Nersha is a prominent rising DJ originating from Birmingham, who is fast capturing attention in the contemporary UK club scene.

While exploring the Birmingham D&B scene, it's also interesting to note the contributions of artists from other parts of the UK. For example, DJ Brockie, real name Steven Altius, is a quintessential figure of the London underground music scene. He is a founding pioneer of jungle and drum & bass, most famous for co-founding London's legendary pirate radio station, Kool FM, in 1991. Brockie's partnership with MC Det is particularly notable, and their synergy in the jungle and D&B scenes has been well-documented.
