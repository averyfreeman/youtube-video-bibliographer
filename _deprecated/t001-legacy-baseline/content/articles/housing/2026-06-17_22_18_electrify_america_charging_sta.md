Electrify America Charging Station Webinar for Property Owners
===========================================================

As a property owner, you may be interested in learning about the opportunities and benefits of hosting an Electrify America charging station. While Electrify America does not currently offer generic, large-scale educational webinars for property owners, they do provide a range of resources and support for those looking to add EV infrastructure to their properties.

For commercial and business site hosts, Electrify America's commercial business unit offers turnkey solutions to build and operate customized electric vehicle (EV) networks. This includes:

* Site feasibility and permitting
* Construction and commissioning
* Ongoing maintenance and operations

By hosting an Electrify America charging station, property owners can establish a new revenue stream and attract EV drivers to their location. Property owners who wish to own and brand the charging stations can do so, with Electrify Commercial offering custom site design, equipment ownership, and grant funding application support.

To be considered for hosting a station, properties must meet certain baseline requirements, including:

* Scale: Ideal sites have the capacity to build Hyper-Fast charging stations with 10 or more chargers.
* Accessibility: Properties must be convenient, high-traffic areas with 24/7 public access, such as retail shopping centers, grocery stores, or hotels.

For those interested in exploring the details of EV charging projects, Electrify America provides a range of resources, including:

* The Electrify America Site Host Contact Form, which allows property owners to directly reach out to their team for more information.
* Electrify America Business Solutions, which provides information on ownership models and network branding for specific business or municipal use-cases.

Financial Yield from Hosting an Electrify America Charging Station
----------------------------------------------------------------

The financial yield from hosting an Electrify America charging station depends heavily on the business model chosen. Property owners can expect returns through either fixed lease payments or variable profit margins.

### Land Lease Model (Zero Capital Expenditure)

In a tenant-lease model, Electrify America rents the property owner's parking spaces and covers 100% of the equipment, installation, and utility costs. In this model, property owners can expect to receive:

* $1,000 to $2,500 per parking space annually
* Up to $24,000 annually for a central footprint in premier, high-traffic metropolitan markets

### Owner-Operator / Revenue-Share Model

If a property owner applies for grant funding, purchases the hardware, and utilizes Electrify Commercial to manage the software and branding, they take on the electricity costs and operational risk. In this model, an average DC Fast Charging (Level 3) station can yield:

* $20,000 to $50,000 in average annual earnings
* A 15% to 30% net profit margin after accounting for network fees, maintenance, and electricity demand charges
* A 5 to 10-year payback period to recoup the initial capital expenditure

### Indirect Yield (The "Dwell Time" Multiplier)

For retail property owners, the direct charging revenue is often secondary to the secondary spending driven by EV drivers. Because Hyper-Fast charging sessions take 20 to 30 minutes, drivers are converted into captive retail customers, leading to increased foot traffic and higher spending at adjacent businesses.

To calculate the exact potential for a property, it's essential to consider factors such as electricity demand charges, the federal 30C tax credit, and average utilization rates. By understanding these factors, property owners can make informed decisions about hosting an Electrify America charging station and maximizing their returns.
