### Comparison of THC and Nicotine Vaping

The following table highlights the differences between THC and nicotine vaping:

| Feature | THC Vaping (Oil-Based) | Nicotine Vaping (Water-Soluble Base) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary Base** | Medium-chain triglyceride (MCT) oil, Vitamin E acetate, or terpenes | Propylene glycol (PG) and vegetable glycerin (VG) |
| **Acute Risk** | **Lipoid Pneumonia**: Lungs cannot clear oil droplets, leading to macrophages engulfing the fat and triggering massive inflammation | **Chemical Pneumonitis**: Acute irritation or allergic response to flavoring chemicals or hyper-concentrated nicotine |
| **Long-Term Risk** | Chronic localized inflammatory responses; less data exists on long-term exclusive legal THC use | **Airway Remodeling**: Chronic irritation from PG/VG and flavorings linked to increased asthma severity and chronic bronchitis |

### Conclusion

The blanket term "vaping" is a catch-all for a device type rather than a specific chemical exposure. In scientific literature, treating an oil-induced lipid injury (THC) and a water-soluble chemical irritant injury (nicotine) as identical is inaccurate. It is crucial to understand the chemical differences between THC and nicotine vaping to accurately assess the risks associated with each.

### Resources for Quitting and Support

If you or someone you know is experiencing respiratory symptoms or needs help stopping vaping, use the following vetted resources to connect with healthcare professionals and cessation tools:

* The American Lung Association: Explore their [E-Cigarettes and Lung Health](https://www.lung.org/quit-smoking/e-cigarettes-vaping/lung-health) page for in-depth educational resources and cessation programs.
* Truth Initiative: Access their text-based [BecomeAnEX](https://www.becomeanex.org/) platform for specialized tools and support designed to quit vaping.
* Medical Guidance: If you vape and are experiencing chronic cough, shortness of breath, or chest pain, consult with a pulmonologist to undergo lung function testing or imaging.
