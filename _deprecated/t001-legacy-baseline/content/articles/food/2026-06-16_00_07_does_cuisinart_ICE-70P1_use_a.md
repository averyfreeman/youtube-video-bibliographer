## Understanding Cuisinart Ice Cream Makers: Compressor vs. Pre‑Frozen Bowl

### Does the Cuisinart ICE‑70P1 Use a Compressor?

The Cuisinart ICE‑70P1 does **not** contain a built‑in compressor. Instead, it relies on a **double‑insulated freezer bowl** that must be frozen in your home freezer for several hours before you can churn ice cream.  

Key implications of the pre‑frozen bowl design:

- **Lighter and more compact** than compressor models.  
- **Lower purchase price** because the cooling system is omitted.  
- Requires **pre‑freezing time**, so you cannot produce back‑to‑back batches without waiting for the bowl to refreeze.

If you need a model that can churn consecutive batches without pre‑freezing, consider a compressor‑equipped unit such as the **Cuisinart ICE‑100**.

---

### Does the Cuisinart ICE‑100 Have a Gelato Mode?

The ICE‑100 does not feature a digital “gelato mode” button on its control panel. Instead, it provides a **dedicated gelato paddle** that changes the machine’s behavior mechanically:

| Paddle | Design | Effect on Mix |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| **Gelato Paddle** | Slower rotation, higher drag | Incorporates less air, yielding a dense, smooth, intensely flavored gelato or sorbet texture |
| **Ice Cream Paddle** | Faster churn, more aeration | Produces a lighter, fluffier traditional ice cream |

To make gelato with the ICE‑100, simply:

1. Install the gelato paddle.  
2. Pour in your prepared base.  
3. Set the standard 60‑minute countdown timer.

Some newer compressor machines use electronic controls to adjust paddle speed, offering a digital “gelato” button. The ICE‑100 achieves the same result through its physical paddle design.

### Choosing the Right Model for Your Kitchen

- **If you prefer a budget‑friendly, space‑saving unit** and don’t mind pre‑freezing the bowl, the **ICE‑70P1** is a solid choice.  
- **If you want continuous batch capability** and prefer a mechanical gelato option without digital mode settings, the **ICE‑100** fits those needs.  
- **If you value electronic mode selection** (e.g., a dedicated gelato button that adjusts paddle speed automatically), look at competitor compressor models that incorporate digital controls.
