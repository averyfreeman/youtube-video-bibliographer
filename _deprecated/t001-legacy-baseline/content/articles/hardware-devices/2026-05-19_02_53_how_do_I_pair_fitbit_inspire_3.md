## Pairing Fitbit Inspire 3
To pair your Fitbit Inspire 3, ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone and plug your tracker into its charger. Open the Fitbit App, tap your profile icon, select Set Up a Device, choose Inspire 3, and enter the 4-digit code displayed on the tracker screen.

## Step-by-Step Pairing Guide
The pairing process involves several straightforward steps:
* Charge your device: Connect your Inspire 3 to the charging cable. The screen will prompt you to download the app.
* Download the app: If you haven't already, download and open the Fitbit App via the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android).
* Log in or sign up: Open the app and sign in to your Fitbit account, or create a new one.
* Add the tracker: Tap the device icon (or your profile picture) in the top-left corner, select Add Device, and choose Inspire 3 from the list.
* Follow the prompts: Follow the on-screen instructions to accept terms and grant necessary location/Bluetooth permissions.
* Enter the pairing code: When the Inspire 3 displays a 4-digit numeric code on its screen, type it into the prompt on your phone's app.
* Complete setup: Wait for the devices to connect. Once finished, the app will walk you through any available firmware updates and basic features.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues
If the app fails to find your Inspire 3, try these quick fixes:
* Restart the tracker: Swipe down on the Inspire 3 clock screen, tap Settings, scroll up to Restart Device, and confirm.
* Check Bluetooth: Turn your phone's Bluetooth off, wait 10 seconds, and turn it back on.
* Clear connections: Make sure your Inspire 3 is not actively connected to any other nearby phones or Bluetooth devices.

## Managing Sleep Mode
To wake your Fitbit Inspire 3 from sleep mode, press and hold the buttons on both sides of the tracker for a few seconds, or firmly tap the screen twice. You can also turn off sleep mode manually:
* Wake the screen: Press both side buttons simultaneously.
* Open Quick Settings: Swipe down from the top of the clock face.
* Locate Sleep Mode: Swipe through the icons until you see a crescent moon icon.
* Turn it off: Tap the icon to turn it from illuminated (On) to dimmed (Off).

## Troubleshooting Screen Unresponsiveness
If your screen becomes unresponsive, try the following:
* Check the charger: Plug the tracker into its charging cable for a few seconds to kick it out of an unresponsive state.
* Force a restart: If the screen remains black, press and hold the side buttons for 10 seconds while it is plugged into the charger until you see the Fitbit logo.
* Adjust settings in the app: Open the Fitbit app on your phone, tap your device icon, and verify your Quiet Modes or sleep schedule settings aren't forcing the device back into sleep mode automatically.
