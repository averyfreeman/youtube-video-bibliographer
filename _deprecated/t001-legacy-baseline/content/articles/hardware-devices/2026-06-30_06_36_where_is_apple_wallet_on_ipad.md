# Managing Apple Wallet on iPad and Mac
As an iPad user, you may have noticed that there is no dedicated Wallet app on the home screen. This is because iPads lack the NFC hardware needed for physical, contactless payments. However, you can still manage your payment cards and use Apple Pay for online or in-app purchases directly from the main Settings app.

## Accessing Wallet Settings on iPad
To access your Wallet settings on your iPad, follow these steps:

1. Open the Settings app on your iPad.
2. Scroll down the left sidebar and tap Wallet & Apple Pay.
3. Here, you can add new debit or credit cards, view your transaction history, and set your default payment or shipping information.

For detailed step-by-step guides on adding and managing your cards, you can refer to the Set up Apple Pay on iPad Guide.

## Managing Apple Pay on Mac
The process of managing Apple Pay on Mac is similar to that on iPad, but with slightly different interface names. Since macOS also lacks a standalone Wallet app, your Apple Card and Apple Pay details live inside System Settings.

## Paying Your Apple Card Balance
If you need to pay your Apple Card balance, you can do so directly from your iPad or Mac. Here are the steps:

### Method 1: Pay using your iPad
1. Open the Settings app.
2. Tap Wallet & Apple Pay in the sidebar.
3. Select your Apple Card.
4. Tap Info, then tap Make a Payment.
5. Choose your payment amount and source to complete the process.

### Method 2: Pay using your Mac
1. Click the Apple Menu (🍏) in the top-left corner.
2. Select System Settings.
3. Click Wallet & Apple Pay in the sidebar.
4. Click on your Apple Card.
5. Select Make a Payment and follow the prompts.

### Alternative Method: Pay online without a device
If you ever lack access to your iPad or Mac, you can manage your card from any web browser.

1. Navigate to [card.apple.com](http://card.apple.com).
2. Sign in with your Apple ID.
3. Click Payments in the sidebar to schedule or clear your balance.

## Resolving the "Apple Pay Disabled" Error on Mac
If you encounter an error message stating that Apple Pay is disabled due to modified security settings on your Mac, you will not be able to make an Apple Card payment from the payment methods section. Instead, use one of the alternative methods below:

### Step 1: Use the iPad (Recommended)
1. Open Settings on your iPad.
2. Tap Wallet & Apple Pay.
3. Select your Apple Card and tap Make a Payment.

### Step 2: Use the Web Browser
1. Navigate directly to [card.apple.com](http://card.apple.com).
2. Log in with your Apple Account credentials.
3. Click Payments in the left sidebar to pay your balance.

### How to Fix the Mac Security Error
To clear the error on your Mac and use Apple Pay normally again, follow these steps:

1. Open System Settings on your Mac.
2. Go to General > Software Update.
3. Find Automatic Updates and click the Info button (i) next to it.
4. Look for Install Security Responses and system files.
5. If it is turned off, turn it on. If it is already on, turn it off, click Done, open it again, and turn it back on.
6. Restart your Mac completely.

Once your Mac reboots, go back to Wallet & Apple Pay; the error should be resolved, and your Apple Card will reappear.
