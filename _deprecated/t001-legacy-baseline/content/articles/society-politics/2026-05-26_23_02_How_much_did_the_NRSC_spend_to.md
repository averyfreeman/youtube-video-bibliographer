Analyzing the 2026 Texas Senate Primary: NRSC Spending and Election Costs
====================================================================

The 2026 Texas Senate primary has been a highly contested and expensive election battle. As an observer of this race, I have been tracking the spending of the National Republican Senatorial Committee (NRSC) and other involved parties. In this article, I will break down the NRSC's spending and the overall costs of the election.

NRSC Spending
-------------

The NRSC was heavily involved in opposing Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton's Senate campaign. Although the NRSC did not spend money directly as independent expenditures to oppose Paxton, they raised funds through joint fundraising committees with incumbent Sen. John Cornyn, such as the Cornyn Lonestar Victory Fund. This mechanism helped the incumbent outspend Paxton and his supporters by more than 10-to-1. The NRSC's efforts, alongside establishment GOP allies, resulted in roughly $13 million being spent to support Cornyn in the primary.

Total Election Costs
--------------------

The total cost of the entire primary race has been reported to be around $135 million, making it the most expensive Senate primary in history. This figure represents the aggregate spending of the entire election battle, including John Cornyn's campaign, Ken Paxton's campaign, and all allied Super PACs. The majority of the spending, over $110 million, came from Senator John Cornyn's campaign and his allies, including the NRSC and major outside groups like Texans for a Conservative Majority.

Breakdown of Spending
---------------------

As of May 26, 2026, the breakdown of the total spending is as follows:

* Pro-Cornyn/Anti-Paxton Alliance: $110 million+
	+ This includes spending from Senator John Cornyn's campaign and his allies, such as the NRSC and Texans for a Conservative Majority.
* Ken Paxton: $13.5 million
	+ Paxton's campaign has been massively outspent, raising only about $13.5 million total.

Discrepancy in Reported Figures
------------------------------

The discrepancy between the earlier reported figures ($13 million-$60 million) and the final total of $135 million can be attributed to the expensive runoff phase and the fact that earlier reports may have only referred to specific ad buys. The $135 million figure captures the final price tag of the 14-month battle now that the runoff is concluding.

In conclusion, the 2026 Texas Senate primary has been a costly and highly contested election battle. The NRSC's spending, although not directly opposing Paxton, played a significant role in supporting Cornyn's campaign. The total cost of the election, $135 million, makes it the most expensive Senate primary in history.
