Troubleshooting the Garmin Dash Cam Mini 2: Resolving Connectivity Issues
====================================================================

The Garmin Dash Cam Mini 2 is a popular choice for drivers looking to record their journeys, but some users have reported issues with the device dropping its connection to their phone when used in their car. This problem is often caused by Wi-Fi interference from wireless Apple CarPlay or Android Auto, an underpowered 12V adapter, or an out-of-range Bluetooth conflict.

To resolve these connectivity issues, follow these steps:

### 1. Disable Wireless Apple CarPlay or Android Auto

Many modern vehicles use in-car Wi-Fi for wireless CarPlay or Android Auto. When you enter the car, your phone automatically connects to the car's Wi-Fi, severing the Wi-Fi connection required to view live video or download files from your Garmin Dash Cam. To fix this, temporarily turn off Wi-Fi on your phone or disconnect CarPlay/Android Auto on your vehicle's infotainment screen. This allows your phone to communicate directly with the dash cam's local Wi-Fi.

### 2. Check Your Power Source

If you test the camera inside using a USB cable connected to a computer, the dash cam only receives enough power to act as a mass storage device, not to transmit data over Wi-Fi. Ensure the camera is plugged into a standard 12V cigarette lighter/power outlet inside your vehicle using the original Garmin vehicle power cable and adapter.

### 3. Forget and Re-Pair Devices

Bluetooth pairing data can get confused when switching between a stationary home network and a moving vehicle network. To resolve this, go to your phone's Bluetooth settings and select "Forget Device" or "Unpair" the Garmin Dash Cam. Open the Garmin Drive App, go to your devices, and select "Forget this Phone" or "Remove Device." Restart both the dash cam and your phone, then re-pair them as brand new devices.
