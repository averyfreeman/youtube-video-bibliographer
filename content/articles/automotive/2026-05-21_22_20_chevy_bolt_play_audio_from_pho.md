Playing Audio from Your Phone in a Chevy Bolt
==============================================

To play audio from your phone in a Chevy Bolt, you must manually select the Bluetooth source on the infotainment screen or use a wired connection for automatic playback. The system typically defaults to the last-used radio source (AM/FM or SiriusXM) upon startup rather than automatically resuming Bluetooth audio.

### Connection Methods

#### 1. Bluetooth Audio (Standard Wireless)

To hear phone audio via Bluetooth, the infotainment system must be explicitly set to that input:

* Set Source: On the touchscreen, tap Audio > Source > Bluetooth.
* Startup Tip: If you find the radio blaring before your phone connects, set the radio source to SiriusXM Channel 0, which provides silence while the Bluetooth link establishes.
* Resume Playback: Most Android devices require you to manually press Play on the phone or car screen even after the connection is established.

#### 2. Android Auto & Apple CarPlay

These services often offer more automatic behavior than standard Bluetooth:

* Wired: Plugging into the USB port typically triggers the car to switch sources automatically.
* Wireless: Available on 2022+ Bolt models; the system often switches to the phone interface as soon as it recognizes the wireless projection.
* Shortcuts: A long press of the Home button on the dashboard will toggle between the native Chevy MyLink/Infotainment screen and the CarPlay/Android Auto interface.

### Troubleshooting No Sound

If your phone is connected but no audio plays:

* Check Profiles: Ensure both Phone Audio and Media Audio are toggled ON in your phone's Bluetooth settings for the car.
* System Reset: If Bluetooth half-connects (calls work but music doesn't), hold the Fast Forward and Home buttons simultaneously to restart the infotainment system.
* Output Priority: In your phone's control center, verify that the audio output is set to My Chevrolet and not the phone's internal speakers.
