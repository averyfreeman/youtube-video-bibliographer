# Troubleshooting Google Pixel 10 Wallet Issues
As a Google Pixel 10 user, you may encounter issues with adding cards to Google Wallet. This article will guide you through the troubleshooting steps to resolve the error and also provide instructions on how to use PayPal as the default payment app instead of Google Wallet.

## Troubleshooting Steps for Google Pixel 10 Wallet Issues
To fix the issue where your Pixel 10 cannot add cards to Google Wallet, follow these structured troubleshooting steps:

1. **Disable Biometrics Temporarily**: A common bug on the Pixel 10 creates a conflict between the phone's biometric security and the card encryption token during initial setup. To resolve this, go to your device Settings > Security & privacy > Device unlock, remove or disable both Face Unlock and Fingerprint Unlock, and leave your security set to PIN or Password only. Then, open the Google Wallet app and attempt to add your cards again. Once your cards are successfully provisioned, you can safely re-enable your fingerprints and Face Unlock.
2. **Check Device Play Protect Certification**: Google Wallet will block all card additions if it detects that the phone's software status is out of sync or uncertified. Open the Google Play Store app, tap your Profile icon in the top right corner > Settings > About, and look under Play Protect certification. If it says Device is not certified, tap Fix this issue or check for a pending Google Play System Update via your main device settings.
3. **Clear Google Services Data**: Corrupted residual cache files from your old phone's data transfer can block new wallet registrations. Go to Settings > Apps > See all apps, find and tap on Google Play services, tap Storage & cache > Clear Storage (or Manage Space) > Clear all data. Repeat this exact clearing process for the Google Wallet app itself, and then restart your phone and attempt to add the cards.
4. **Contact Your Bank to Reset "Tokens"**: If you migrated to the Pixel 10 from an older device, your bank may be blocking the card because it thinks it is still assigned to your old phone's security hardware. Call your bank's customer support line (usually found on the back of the physical card) and inform them: "I am trying to add my card to Google Wallet on a new device, and it fails. Please reset or clear the digital wallet tokens for this card."

## Using PayPal as the Default Payment App
To use PayPal as your default tap-and-pay method instead of Google Wallet, follow these steps:

1. **Set PayPal as Default NFC App**: Open your Pixel 10 Settings app, tap Connected devices > Connection preferences, tap NFC > Contactless payments, tap Default payment app, and select PayPal from the list of options.
2. **Configure the PayPal App**: Open the PayPal app on your phone, log into your account, tap your Profile icon or go to the Wallet tab, look for the Set up tap and pay (or In-store payments) option, and select the specific debit card, credit card, or bank balance you want PayPal to charge when you tap your phone.
3. **Change "Use Default" Setting (Optional)**: While still inside the Contactless payments menu in your phone settings, look for the Use default app setting. You can set it to Always if you want PayPal to handle every single tap, even if Google Wallet is open on your screen. Alternatively, set it to Except when another payment app is open if you want PayPal as the main option, but still want the flexibility to manually open Google Wallet for certain purchases.

Note that if the PayPal option is missing from your Contactless payments list, or if you are looking to link PayPal inside Google Wallet instead, or replace Google Wallet entirely, you may need to explore additional configuration options or contact PayPal support for further assistance.
