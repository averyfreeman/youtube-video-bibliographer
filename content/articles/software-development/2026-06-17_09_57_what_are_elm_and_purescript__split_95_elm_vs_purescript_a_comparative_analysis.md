In conclusion, both Elm and PureScript are powerful functional programming languages that can be used for web development. While they share some similarities, they also have some key differences. Elm is a more opinionated language that is designed specifically for front-end web development, while PureScript is a more general-purpose language that can be used for both front-end and back-end development. Ultimately, the choice between Elm and PureScript will depend on your specific needs and preferences.
