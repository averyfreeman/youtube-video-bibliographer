## The Search for a Perfect Turntable
For those seeking a turntable that replicates the feel of a vintage Technics SL-1200MK2, there are several options to consider:
* The Technics SL-1200MK7 and GR2, which offer a similar layout and build quality
* The Pioneer DJ PLX-1000, which is a close aesthetic and ergonomic clone of the SL-1200MK2
* The Reloop RP-7000 MK2, which is a highly praised alternative with adjustable motor torque

## Community Sentiment and Comparative Evaluation
A comprehensive breakdown of community sentiment and technical attributes reveals the strengths and weaknesses of each turntable model:
* Technics SL-1200MK7: divisive, with a lighter weight and criticism of the dropped heavy, vibration-dampening rubber base
* Technics SL-1200GR2: revered but expensive, with a heavy, flawless build quality
* Pioneer DJ PLX-1000: praised for its immense weight, layout familiarity, and road-ready isolation
* Reloop RP-7000 MK2: widely considered the best value-for-money turntable, with customizable torque and a high-quality build

## Category Winners
The winners in each category are:
* Isolation & Chassis Dampening: Technics SL-1200GR2
* Platter "Nudge" & Drag Feel: Reloop RP-7000 MK2 and Technics SL-1200GR2 (tie)
* Pitch Fader Intuition: Technics SL-1200GR2
* Value for Money: Reloop RP-7000 MK2

## The Conundrum: Are Reloop's Upgrades Actually Negatives?
For an SL-1200MK2 purist, the upgrades on the Reloop RP-7000 MK2 can feel like negatives, including:
* The torque trap, which requires a lighter touch
* The smooth digital fader, which lacks the mechanical click or centering resistance of the vintage analog pitch assembly

## Overall Winner for an MK2 Purist
The overall winner for an MK2 purist is the Technics SL-1200GR2, which feels like an authentic piece of heavy machinery rather than a consumer electronics device.

## Alternative Avenues
If you're interested in alternative avenues, consider:
* Buying a professionally refurbished pair of original vintage MK2s
* Using a built-in phono pre-amp
* Exploring the Rane Twelve MKII, a motorized controller with no tonearm

## The Rane Twelve MKII
The Rane Twelve MKII is a 12-inch motorized turntable with no tonearm, using a real high-torque motor and a real 12-inch vinyl platter to send high-resolution MIDI data directly to your laptop via USB.

## The Reloop RP-8000 MK2
The Reloop RP-8000 MK2 is a hybrid turntable that uses DVS (Digital Vinyl System) for audio tracking, requiring a control record on the platter and a needle to read the timecode.

## Phase DJ
Phase DJ is a small wireless sensor that sits on the spindle of any turntable, allowing you to buy a real Technics 1200 and never install a cartridge or needle, while retaining the option to play real vinyl later.

## Recommendation
Based on your specific needs and preferences:
* If you want zero maintenance and will never play real vinyl, get the Rane Twelve MKII.
* If you want the exact MK2 feel, get a Technics SL-1200MK7 (or find used MK2s) and buy a Phase kit.
* If you're interested in a hybrid turntable, consider the Reloop RP-8000 MK2, but be aware of its limitations and requirements.
