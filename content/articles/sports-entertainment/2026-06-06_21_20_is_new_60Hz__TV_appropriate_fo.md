Using a 60Hz+ TV as a Monitor in 2026: A Guide
=====================================================

In 2026, modern 60Hz+ TVs have become a viable option for use as PC monitors, as the technological gap between TVs and monitors has significantly narrowed. However, the suitability of a TV as a monitor depends on specific needs, making it essential to choose the right panel and specs.

### Key Considerations

When selecting a TV to use as a monitor, several factors come into play:

* **Productivity & Text Clarity**: For general office work and coding, a 4K resolution screen is recommended. Lower resolutions, such as 1080p or 1440p, may appear fuzzy on large TV screens when viewed up close.
* **Size & Distance**: It's crucial to avoid choosing a TV that's too large. For a desktop setup, screen sizes typically cap out at 42 to 48 inches. A deep desk is necessary to maintain a comfortable viewing distance.
* **Gaming & High Refresh Rates**: For fast-paced gaming, it's best to avoid basic 60Hz screens. Instead, opt for 120Hz to 144Hz TVs, which provide smooth motion and responsive gameplay.
* **Input Lag**: Modern TVs often feature dedicated "Game Modes" that reduce latency and input lag, making them as responsive as standard monitors.
* **Graphics & Cables**: To achieve 4K at 120Hz+ with HDR enabled, a graphics card with an HDMI 2.1 port and a certified HDMI 2.1 cable are required.

### Top TV-as-Monitor Picks

Some notable TV models suitable for use as monitors include:

* **Best Overall Compact OLED**: The LG C4 Series, starting at 42 inches, offers an exceptional desktop OLED experience with 120Hz and deep blacks.
* **Best for Demanding PCs**: The Samsung S95D/S90F QD-OLED Series provides stellar brightness and refresh rates up to 144Hz, ideal for immersive gaming.

### Technical Tips for Your Setup

To optimize your TV-as-monitor setup:

* **Bypass Smart Menus**: Navigate to Settings > System > Power > Power On and select the specific HDMI port your PC is connected to.
* **Text Clarity**: Ensure your PC output is set to 4K (3840 x 2160) to maintain clear text and images.
* **Power Outage Behavior**: Note that features like "Fast TV Start" can cause the TV to turn on automatically after a power flicker. If this is annoying, you can disable the feature in the power settings, though it will result in a longer boot time.

By considering these factors and tips, you can find a suitable TV to use as a monitor for your office work or gaming needs, even on a tight budget.
