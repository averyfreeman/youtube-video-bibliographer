Controlling Mixxx with Gemini MDJ Players and Other Large-Format Controllers
====================================================================

As a digital DJ, I often explore various hardware options to enhance my performance. In this article, I will discuss how to control Mixxx with Gemini MDJ players and other large-format separate platter controllers, media players, or CD players.

### Using Gemini MDJ Players with Mixxx

Gemini MDJ players, such as the MDJ-500, MDJ-900, and MDJ-1000, can be used to control Mixxx, but they require manual configuration. Since Mixxx does not ship with official, built-in mappings for these specific media players, I need to set them up using their MIDI/HID capabilities.

To connect and map my Gemini MDJ players, I follow these steps:

* Connect via USB: I plug the MDJ players directly into my computer via USB and set them to "MIDI Mode" in the player's utility settings.
* Avoid Name Conflicts: If I am using two of the same MDJ players, I change the MIDI channel (e.g., set one to channel 1 and the other to channel 2) in the device's hardware settings to avoid conflicts.
* Controller Mapping: I map the buttons, platters, and faders myself using Mixxx's Learn wizard or download community-built mapping scripts from the Mixxx Controller Mapping Forum.

However, there are some limitations to consider:

* Display Screens: While I can successfully map the standard transport controls (play, cue, tempo faders, etc.), it is currently not possible to send track data or waveform visuals from the computer back to the screens on the MDJ players due to Mixxx mapping limitations.
* Community Support: Because Gemini MDJ mappings are provided by volunteers rather than officially certified by the Mixxx team, some features (like slip mode or specific LED feedback) may require minor tweaking in the script files.
