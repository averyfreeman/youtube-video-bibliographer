Using the Pixel Health App for Food Tracking
==============================================

The Google Health app, formerly Fitbit, can be used for food and calorie tracking. Key features include manual entry, barcode scanning, photo and conversational logging for premium subscribers, and third-party syncing with external food-logging apps like MyFitnessPal.

Things to Keep in Mind
------------------------

*   Food logging is currently phone-only, with no standalone app or tile for Wear OS on your Pixel Watch.
*   Some food database entries can be clunky, although updates have continually improved macros tracking.

Achieving Dietary Goals with the Google Health App
-------------------------------------------------

While the Google Health app does not have meal planning features, it does track macronutrients like carbohydrates, fats, and protein. To achieve dietary goals, such as a 1500-calorie keto target with 1-2 evening meals, you can use the app to log and verify daily macros.

### Macronutrient Tracking

The app automatically calculates daily grams of carbs, fats, and protein as you log your food.

### Net Carb Calculation

The app displays total fiber and total carbohydrates. You will need to manually subtract fiber from total carbohydrates to ensure you stay under your 50-gram limit.

### No Built-In Meal Planning

The app cannot generate meal plans, suggest recipes, or build shopping lists.

Instant Pot Keto Beef Noodle Soup (with Tendon & Tripe)
------------------------------------------------------

This batch recipe yields approximately 6 generous servings.

| Component | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary Proteins | 1 lb Beef Tendon, 1 lb Beef Tripe, 2 lbs Oxtail (or Brisket/Shank) | Oxtail adds rich fat and flavor. |
| Keto Aromatics | 2" Ginger (sliced), 8 Garlic cloves, 1 Onion (halved) | Ginger and garlic are essential low-carb bases. |
| Low-Carb Veggies | 600g Daikon radish, 130g Carrots, 200g Mushrooms | Daikon is a great low-carb potato substitute. |
| Liquid & Spices | 8-10 cups Water/Beef Stock, Spice Ball (Star anise, cinnamon, bay leaves) | Stock provides a richer base than plain water. |
| Seasoning | 1/2 cup Sugarless Soy Sauce, 1/3 cup Rice Wine (zero carb) | Rice wine and sugarless soy sauce are keto-compliant. |

Portioning and Yield
--------------------

*   **Total Volume**: This recipe yields roughly 12-14 cups of liquid plus approximately 2.5–3 lbs of cooked solids.
*   **Serving Size**: One large, "healthy appetite" serving is 1/6 (one-sixth) of the total cooked volume.
*   **Individual Bowl Breakdown**: A standard generous bowl consists of approximately 4–5 oz of assorted meats, 1.5 cups of konjac noodles, and 2 cups of broth.

Cooking Instructions
--------------------

1.  **Blanch**: Place tendon, tripe, and oxtail in the Instant Pot with water. Use the "Saute" function to boil for 5 minutes, then drain and rinse to remove impurities.
2.  **Sauté Aromatics**: Sauté garlic, onions, and ginger in the pot until fragrant.
3.  **Pressure Cook Meat**: Add the blanched meat, stock, soy sauce, rice wine, and spice ball. Cook on High Pressure for 60-100 minutes (depending on the desired tenderness of the tendon).
4.  **Add Vegetables**: Perform a quick pressure release. Add the daikon, carrots, and mushrooms. Pressure cook for an additional 5-10 minutes or use the "Saute" function until tender.
5.  **Finish**: Stir in your pre-rinsed konjac noodles at the very end just to heat through.

Keto & Nutrition Tracking
-------------------------

*   **Macronutrients**: A typical serving of keto beef noodle soup (with beef shank/tendon) contains approximately 4g–9g net carbs, 32g–52g protein, and 7g–57g fat, depending on the cut of beef used.
*   **Offal Nutrition**: 3 oz of mixed beef tendon and tripe provides roughly 160 calories and is a good source of iron and protein.
*   **Sodium Note**: Since you are limiting salt, use low-sodium beef broth and stick to the specified 1/2 cup of soy sauce for the entire 6-serving batch, as soy sauce is a major sodium source.

Integrating Fresh Turmeric into Your Beef Soup
--------------------------------------------

Fresh turmeric is an excellent choice for a keto diet and fits perfectly within your 50g net carb limit.

### Keto-Friendly Profile of Turmeric

*   **Carb Count**: One tablespoon of ground turmeric contains approximately 3g of net carbs. A standard serving of fresh turmeric root (about 1 inch) has negligible net carbs.
*   **Anti-Inflammatory Benefits**: Turmeric's primary bioactive compound, curcumin, is a strong antioxidant with powerful anti-inflammatory properties.
*   **Ketosis Support**: Some research suggests turmeric can help stabilize insulin levels and reduce inflammation, which supports the body's transition into and maintenance of ketosis.

### Preparation and Enhanced Absorption

*   **Preparation**: Peel and thinly slice or grate about 1 to 2 inches of fresh turmeric root.
*   **Enhanced Absorption**: Curcumin is not well-absorbed by the body on its own. To increase absorption, always consume turmeric with black pepper and a fat source (like the fat from your oxtail).

### Cooking

*   **Add Fresh Turmeric**: Add the fresh turmeric along with the ginger and garlic during the initial sauté step or before pressure cooking to let its flavors meld with the broth.

Including fresh turmeric in your soup will add a unique earthy, peppery flavor and a vibrant yellow color. Since you already use turmeric capsules, adding the fresh root to your meals provides a natural culinary boost to your anti-inflammatory efforts while you recover.
