## Impact Studies of the Obama Presidential Center

### Economic Impact

- **Anderson Economic Group (AEG) Study** – Commissioned by the University of Chicago, the analysis projects roughly **$220 million in annual economic activity** for Chicago and estimates **over $3 billion in cumulative impact** during the center’s first decade.
- **Deloitte Consulting Assessment** – Conducted for the Obama Foundation, this study forecasts **thousands of local jobs** across construction, operations, and visitor services.

### Environmental and Historical Impact

- **Federal Highway Administration NEPA/Section 106 Reviews** – Federal reviews concluded that construction would produce an **“adverse effect” on historic Jackson Park**, originally designed by Frederick Law Olmsted.
- **Migratory Bird Assessments** – The Obama Foundation released mitigation strategies that incorporate **sustainable glass architecture and adjusted lighting** to reduce impacts on migratory bird patterns.

### Community and Gentrification

- **Housing‑Market and Displacement Research** – Academic and community analyses (e.g., the Illinois Answers Project) track the center’s influence on neighborhoods such as Woodlawn. While the project delivers an economic boost, rising property values have **triggered significant gentrification and displacement concerns** for long‑time residents.

Detailed economic figures are available in the **Obama Foundation Economic Impact Assessment**, and community dynamics are explored in the **Frontiers in Sustainable Cities Research**.

## Property‑Tax Relief Initiatives

To shield fixed‑income homeowners from rising taxes linked to gentrification, several programs have been introduced around the Obama Presidential Center:

| Program | Scope | Key Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Woodlawn Housing Ordinance (2020)** | Woodlawn neighborhood | Allocates **> $1 million** for home‑improvement grants and cost‑stabilization for long‑term owners. |
| **Cook County Homeowner Relief Fund** | Cook County | Temporary grants for low‑ and fixed‑income households facing sudden property‑tax spikes. |
| **Community Benefits Agreement (CBA) Coalition** | Citywide | Advocacy campaign urging the city and the Obama Foundation to enact **formal tax freezes** for elderly and fixed‑income residents as surrounding property values climb. |
