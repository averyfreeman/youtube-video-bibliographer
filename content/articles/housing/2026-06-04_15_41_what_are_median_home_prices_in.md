Median Home Prices in Medina, WA
=====================================

Median home prices in Medina, WA typically range from $4.5M to $5.6M, depending on recent sales cycles and seasonal inventory. Hillside non-waterfront homes generally start at $3M, while premier Lake Washington waterfront properties can easily command $10M to $30M+. To better understand the market dynamics, it's essential to consider the following key statistics:

* Median Listing Price: Hovers around $5.4M – $6.28M.
* Price Per Square Foot: Tends to average around $1,300.
* Days on Market: Homes typically spend anywhere from 12 to 49 days on the market, depending on the season.

As one of the wealthiest ZIP codes in the United States, Medina features hyper-luxury properties that often cause month-to-month fluctuations in the median figures. Key Differentiating Factors
---------------------------

Several factors contribute to the significant differences between Medina, WA and Atherton, CA:

* **The Dirt and Zoning Laws**: Atherton's exorbitant pricing is heavily driven by land value and heritage zoning laws. Most of the town requires a one-acre minimum lot size for single-family homes, creating massive, gated country estates. Medina is much more densely clustered, with multi-million dollar homes sitting on significantly smaller pieces of land, though it commands a premium for its private Lake Washington waterfront access.
* **Estate Scale**: The physical size of the homes differs dramatically. Because of the acre-plus lots, the median home size in Atherton hovers around 9,000+ square feet. Medina features a broader mix, ranging from massive luxury custom builds to smaller mid-century properties tucked into the hillsides.
* **Insulated Cash Markets**: While Medina is highly sensitive to local corporate pacing, Atherton operates almost entirely as an all-cash ecosystem for billionaires, VC partners, and international elite. It remains largely decoupled from standard macroeconomic factors or interest rate fluctuations.
