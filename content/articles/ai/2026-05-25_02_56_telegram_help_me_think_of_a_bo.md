Naming Your Telegram Bot: A Guide to Finding the Perfect Name
===========================================================

When it comes to naming your Telegram bot, you have several options to consider. You can stick with the original project's lobster/claw branding, lean into its functional identity as a personal AI agent, or explore other themes. In this article, we'll explore various naming options and provide guidance on how to register your bot's name with Telegram.

### Naming by Theme

#### Lobster & Marine Branding

Since "Clawdbot" uses a lobster emoji 🦞, you can use related terms to create a unique name. Some options include:

* **ClawDaddy** (popularly used as a main orchestrator)
* **Moltbot** or **Molty** (the official rebrand names)
* **MiniClaw** or **CloudClaw**
* **LobbyBot** or **Shelly**

#### Personal & Humanoid

To make the agent feel like a "24/7 teammate," many users choose traditional names. Some options include:

* **Henry**, **Chloe**, or **Quill**
* **Sage**, **Franklin**, or **Dembe**

#### Pop Culture & Sci-Fi

You can also draw inspiration from popular culture and sci-fi. Some options include:

* **Jarvis** (from Iron Man)
* **Hal** (from 2001: A Space Odyssey)
* **Neo** or **Ultron**

### Telegram Technical Constraints

When registering your name with **BotFather**, Telegram's API Manager, remember:

* Display Name: This can be anything (e.g., "My AI Assistant") and can be changed later.
* Username: This must be globally unique and **must end in "bot"** (e.g., `MyCustomClaw_bot`).

### Unique Variations of "Clawdbot"

To guarantee availability, you can use unique prefixes, technical suffixes, or combine the "Clawd/Molt" branding. Some options include:

* **ClawdAgent_bot**
* **ClawdTeammate_bot**
* **OpenClawd_bot**
* **MyClawdInstance_bot**
* **ClawdRunner_bot**

If you prefer to use the "Molt" rebrand, some options include:

* **MoltOrchestrator_bot**

* **MoltCore_bot**

### Suffix Strategies for Guaranteed Availability

If a name you love is taken, appending a small custom modifier before `_bot` satisfies the Telegram API while ensuring nobody else has claimed it. This approach allows you to create a unique name that meets Telegram's requirements.

By considering these options and strategies, you can find the perfect name for your Telegram bot and ensure that it is available for registration.
