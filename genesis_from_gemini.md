## Key Concepts: The Physics of Token Limits

- **The "Reused Input" Trap:** LLMs do not have native memory. Every time you submit a prompt in an ongoing chat, the entire conversation history is bundled and sent back to the model. In a heavy coding session, up to 96% of processed tokens are just the model re-reading the old conversation.
- **Compounding Costs:** Your 1st message costs exactly what you typed. Your 10th message costs what you typed _plus_ the previous nine exchanges. By message 30, your actual prompt is a rounding error compared to the bloat of the context window.
- **The "Messy Desk" Analogy:** More capable models and larger tool integrations (like GitHub, Sentry, or local file search) load massive amounts of hidden instructions and definitions into the context window before you even type a word. You must actively curate what is on your "desk."
- **Double Penalty on Output:** Output tokens are the most expensive to generate, and if you ask for long, verbose answers, you pay for those tokens _again_ on every subsequent turn of the conversation.

### Level 1: Clean Your Own Desk (Manual Habits)

4. **Carry the artifact, not the argument:** When moving to a new step in a workflow, copy _only_ the final generated output (the artifact) into a new chat. Leave behind your bad first drafts, rejected ideas, and the AI's reasoning.
