#### Inspiration:

I was looking through [data.wa.gov](https://data.wa.gov) for a dataset to do a project with, and I saw that the [PDC](https://pdc.wa.gov) has information from lobbyist disclosures on who is funding their lobbying efforts. I thought that was really interesting, so I looked into it more, and one of the things that really struck me was from how much of the money is coming from outside of WA.

I plan to release more charts done with other information from this dataset. I have a blog site with a couple more charts at [developer monkey / unixgreybeard.org](https://develmonk.com/2024/06/12/graphing-numbers-with-geospatial-boundaries-using-geopandas-and-matplotlib/).

![demo of bar_chart.py](https://develmonk.com/images/out_of_state_lobby_money_bar_chart.gif?raw=true "demo of bar_chart.py")

#### TL;DR how do I run these chart generators?

The easiest thing to do is run the chart recorders in a docker image, so dependencies are isolated from your system and the recordings return in both `.gif` and `.mp4` format in the `$(pwd)/recordings` folder.

OCI version is available at [Docker Hub](https://hub.docker.com/r/averyfreeman/wa-lobbbyist-dataset-animations) and requires a `recordings` folder for output.
