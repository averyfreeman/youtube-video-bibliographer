**2. Canal Mania vs. Railway Mania (18th/19th Century)**

- **Reference in Video:** "just like with the railroads the future was the railroads... the canals uh the railroads kind of eclipse the canals"

- **Timestamp:**

- **Link to Original Source:** [Historic UK: Railway Mania](https://www.historic-uk.com/HistoryUK/HistoryofBritain/Railway-Mania/?utm_source=gemini)

- **Bibliographer's Note:** This is a fleeting but accurate reference to two massive, sequential infrastructure bubbles. "Canal Mania" hit the UK in the 1790s, completely transforming transport, only to be entirely eclipsed by "Railway Mania" in the 1840s, which resulted in a massive speculative bubble and subsequent crash, even as it laid the foundational infrastructure for the modern economy.

**3. The Repeal of the Glass-Steagall Act (1999)**

- **Reference in Video:** "in 2008 nobody I can guarantee you at the time of what was it GlassSteagall repeal nobody said a word right nobody nobody was paying attention... But after '08 everybody's like 'How did this happen?'"

- **Timestamp:**

- **Link to Original Source:** [Federal Reserve History: Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act of 1999](https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/gramm-leach-bliley-act?utm_source=gemini)

- **Bibliographer's Note:** The host is referencing the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act of 1999 (signed into law by President Bill Clinton), which repealed the Glass-Steagall Act of 1933. Glass-Steagall originally separated commercial banking from investment banking. Its repeal is widely cited by economic historians as a key catalyst that allowed banks to take on massive risks, directly contributing to the 2008 Great Recession.

**4. Credit Default Swaps & The Subprime Crisis**

- **Reference in Video:** "if you go back to ' 06 nobody knew what a credit default swap was nobody everybody found out in '09 when they're like 'How did this happen?'"

- **Timestamp:**

- **Link to Original Source:** [Investopedia: Credit Default Swaps (CDS)](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/c/creditdefaultswap.asp?utm_source=gemini)

- **Bibliographer's Note:** A Credit Default Swap (CDS) is a financial derivative that essentially acts as insurance on a loan. A lack of regulation in the CDS market allowed financial institutions to obscure massive amounts of risk tied to subprime mortgages, triggering the 2008 global financial meltdown.

**5. Andrew Carnegie & Gilded Age Philanthropy**

- **Reference in Video:** "in the industrialist age where you could make an argument and Carnegie and all this people were building all these temples to their own ego with the libraries"

- **Timestamp:**

- **Link to Original Source:** [Carnegie Corporation: The Story of Carnegie Libraries](https://www.carnegie.org/interactives/foundersstory/mission/?utm_source=gemini)

- **Bibliographer's Note:** The host is referencing industrialist Andrew Carnegie, who amassed a vast fortune in the steel industry during America's Gilded Age. While often criticized for ruthless business practices and anti-labor actions (like the Homestead Strike), Carnegie spent the last phase of his life giving away 90% of his fortune, funding the construction of 2,509 public libraries worldwide.

**6. The Origin of the Progressive Income Tax**

- **Reference in Video:** "the UK was the first to move on a progressive income tax 100 plus years a more than 100 years ago and then that kicked it off around the rest of the world"

- **Timestamp:**

- **Link to Original Source:** [UK Parliament: The People's Budget 1909](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/private-lives/taxation/overview/peoplesbudget/?utm_source=gemini)

- **Bibliographer's Note:** The host is referencing the "People's Budget" of 1909, championed by David Lloyd George, which introduced unprecedented taxes on the wealthy to fund social welfare programs. (Note: The very *first* UK income tax was actually introduced in 1799 by William Pitt the Younger to pay for the Napoleonic Wars, but the host's "100 plus years ago" timeline correctly aligns with the 1909 modern progressive shift). In the US, the 16th Amendment establishing a federal income tax was ratified shortly after, in 1913.
