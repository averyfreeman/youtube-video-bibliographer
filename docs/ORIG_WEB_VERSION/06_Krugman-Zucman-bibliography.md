### Video 1: Fundamental Tensions of Wealth (Zucman & Krugman)

- **Aristotle on Extreme Wealth and Democracy**

  - **Historical Reference:** Aristotle's *Politics* (specifically his writings on oligarchy versus democracy and the destabilizing nature of extreme wealth inequality).

  - **Original Source:** [Internet Classics Archive: Aristotle's *Politics* (Book IV)](http://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/politics.4.four.html?utm_source=gemini)

  - **Video Quote:** "all the thinkers uh of democracy have written about this all the way back to Aristotle more than 2,000 years ago"

  - **Timestamp:** `[00:00:23]` (0 min, 23 sec)

- **The Post-WWII "Great Compression" of Wealth**

  - **Economic Concept:** The period of mid-20th-century wealth equalization driven by war shocks, high taxation, and organized labor.

  - **Original Source:** [NBER: The Great Compression (Goldin & Margo, 1992)](https://www.nber.org/papers/w3817?utm_source=gemini)

  - **Video Quote:** "when extreme wealth had largely disappeared after World War II after the shocks of the first half of the 20th century"

  - **Timestamp:** `[00:00:52]` (0 min, 52 sec)

- **US Household Wealth-to-GDP Ratio (1980 vs. Today)**

  - **Economic Data:** Tracking the total value of assets relative to national economic output.

  - **Original Source:** [Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED): Total Net Worth as Percent of GDP](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/HNONWPDPI?utm_source=gemini)

  - **Video Quote:** "in the US in 1980 it was around 200 250% and today we are past 500%"

  - **Timestamp:** `[00:02:26]` (2 min, 26 sec)

- **Top 1% Income Share Growth (1980 vs. Today)**

  - **Economic Data:** The share of total national income captured by the top percentile.

  - **Original Source:** [World Inequality Database (WID.world)](https://wid.world/?utm_source=gemini)

  - **Video Quote:** "the top 1% share of total income has increased from about 10% of income in the US in 1980 to about 20% today"

  - **Timestamp:** `[00:03:10]` (3 min, 10 sec)

- **Top 0.001% (Billionaire) Wealth Share**

  - **Economic Data:** The concentration of total national wealth held by the absolute top fraction of a percent.

  - **Original Source:** [ProPublica / Zucman Wealth Data Project](https://www.propublica.org/article/the-secret-irs-files-trove-of-never-before-seen-records-reveal-how-the-wealthiest-avoid-income-tax?utm_source=gemini)

  - **Video Quote:** "if you look at billionaires roughly the top 0.001% of the population um they own about 7% of total US wealth today in 1980 they used to own about 1%"

  - **Timestamp:** `[00:04:36]` (4 min, 36 sec)

  - **Economic Data:** The wealth of the top 20 individuals relative to total US GDP.

- **Billionaire Political Spending (2024 Election Cycle)**
